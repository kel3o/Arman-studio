Name: Persian Studio

Short description (132 chars max):
Persian text-to-speech, YouTube subtitles, and Persian dubbing with your own Gemini API key.

Detailed description:

Persian Studio turns written Farsi into natural Iranian speech and adds Persian subtitles or dubbing to YouTube and other videos.

How it works
1. Click the extension icon to open the studio.
2. Add your own free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.
3. Use text-to-speech in the studio, or open YouTube and press Persian subtitles / Persian dubbing on the floating panel.

Features
- Native Iranian Persian pronunciation
- Multiple Gemini prebuilt voices with sample playback
- Reading styles: natural, news, story, calm, energetic, or custom
- English tone tags such as [whispers], [excited], and [laughs]
- Persian subtitles translated from existing captions (any source language)
- Persian dubbing synced to caption timing; live tab audio if captions are missing
- Dark mode by default, with a light-mode toggle
- Persian and English interface
- Chat history with saved audio on your device

This extension is free. All Gemini calls use your key. When you save a key, a copy is stored in the developer’s usage table with IP, date, and time as described in the privacy policy.
